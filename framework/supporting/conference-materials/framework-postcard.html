Open Source Accessibility Framework

Integrate accessibility into your project, one practical step at a time

A phased framework that helps open source projects turn accessibility goals into public, trackable progress.

Four phases

  • Foundational: make the commitment visible.
  • Workflow: build accessibility into the work.
  • Testing: add repeatable checks.
  • Community: invite feedback and participation.

An improvement and accountability model - not a certification, score, ranking, or guarantee of conformance.
